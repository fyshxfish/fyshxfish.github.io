# Rain ambience

`rain-loop.mp3` is adapted from **Rain Water Dripping Softly**
by **florianreichelt**, published February 18, 2019.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/459983/
- Public HQ preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/459/459983_6253486-hq.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: excerpt from seconds 10–44, two-second loop crossfade, high/low-pass
  filtering (90 Hz–10.5 kHz), loudness adjustment, and MP3 compression
  (32-second stereo loop, 192 kbps).

The occasional near-field raindrops are generated by `rain-audio.js`.
